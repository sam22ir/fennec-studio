# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Algeria and potential collaborators encountering Fennec Studio for the first time. They need to understand the studio's local point of view, current work, and practical purpose quickly in either English or Arabic.

## Product Purpose

Present Fennec as an independent Algerian digital-product studio that reduces everyday friction. Success means visitors leave with a clear sense of the studio's values, products, and local credibility, then feel confident starting a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Local, direct, purposeful. The experience should feel confident and distinctly Algerian without becoming ceremonial, nostalgic, or ornamental.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic imported SaaS aesthetics, decorative technology imagery, mascot-style cartoon treatment, cleverness without utility, and motion that competes with the message.

## Design Principles

- Local by design: use Algeria as lived context, not as surface decoration.
- Clear at every step: favor direct language and legible interaction.
- Useful before clever: visual ideas must support comprehension or identity.
- One strong gesture: let a focused brand moment carry more weight than scattered effects.
- Bilingual by default: English and Arabic should feel equally intentional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep content usable across mobile and desktop, preserve semantic structure and keyboard access, maintain readable contrast, and provide a stable non-animated presentation when reduced motion is requested.
